---------
Noto Serif Bengali 3.000, Copyright 2025 The Noto Project Authors
(https://github.com/notofonts/bengali). Designed by Juan Bruce, Universal Thirst,
Indian Type Foundry and the Monotype Design Team.

Changelog
---------
12 November 2026 - Version 3.100 (Noto Serif by Gunjoron)
- New Bengali digits U+09E6-U+09EF (০১২৩৪৫৬৭৮৯), drawn for Gunjoron by
  Mustafizur Rahman Junayed in all 9 weights (Thin to Black). In each weight
  they are scaled and positioned to match the Noto text, and their spacing is
  set to Noto's average.
- Family renamed to "Noto Serif by Gunjoron" (PostScript: NotoSerifByGunjoron-*).
- Updated the copyright, version, publisher, creator, description and URL
  metadata, and the vendor ID (GUNJ).
- Nothing else was changed. Every other glyph, its metrics, the Bengali shaping
  (GSUB/GPOS/GDEF) and the hinting are byte-for-byte identical to
  Noto Serif Bengali 3.000.
